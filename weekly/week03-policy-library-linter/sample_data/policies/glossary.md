# Maple Ridge Credit Union — Information Security Glossary

The single source of defined terms for all policies, standards and procedures. A policy
that needs a term should use this definition, not write its own.

- **Information Asset**: Any information, or system that stores, processes or transmits information, that has value to the credit union.
- **Security Incident**: An event that actually or potentially compromises the confidentiality, integrity or availability of an Information Asset, or breaches a security policy.
- **Personal Information**: Information about an identifiable individual, as defined in PIPEDA.
- **Privileged Account**: An account that can change system configuration, security settings or other users' access.
- **Multi-Factor Authentication** (MFA): Authentication using two or more independent factors (something you know, have or are).
- **Chief Information Security Officer** (CISO): Executive accountable for the information security program.
- **Chief Risk Officer** (CRO): Executive accountable for enterprise risk management.
- **Business Impact Analysis** (BIA): Assessment of the impact over time of disrupting a business process.
- **Recovery Time Objective** (RTO): Maximum acceptable time to restore a process after disruption.
- **Recovery Point Objective** (RPO): Maximum acceptable data loss, measured in time.
- **Virtual Private Network** (VPN): An encrypted tunnel between a remote device and the corporate network.
- **Information Technology** (IT): The credit union's technology function.
- **Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions** (OSFI): Canada's federal prudential regulator.
- **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act** (PIPEDA): Canada's federal private-sector privacy law.
- **International Organization for Standardization** (ISO): Standards body; with IEC, publisher of ISO/IEC 27001.
- **International Electrotechnical Commission** (IEC): Standards body; co-publisher of ISO/IEC 27001.
- **National Institute of Standards and Technology** (NIST): United States standards body; publisher of the Cybersecurity Framework.
- **Human Resources** (HR): The credit union's people function.
- **Chief Executive Officer** (CEO): The credit union's most senior executive.
- **Transport Layer Security** (TLS): Protocol that encrypts data in transit.
- **Advanced Encryption Standard** (AES): Approved symmetric encryption algorithm.
- **Secure Hash Algorithm** (SHA): Family of cryptographic hash functions.
- **MD5**: Message Digest 5, an obsolete hash function.
- **Information Asset**: Data or equipment owned by the credit union.
