# Data Classification and Handling Policy (DRAFT)

## Purpose

To classify credit union information by sensitivity so that it is handled consistently.

## Scope

All information created, received or held by the credit union, in any format.

## Policy statements

1. Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted.
2. Member Personal Information is Confidential at minimum.
3. Card data and authentication data are Restricted.
4. Retention periods for each class: TBD with Legal and the FINTRAC compliance officer.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Information owners** classify their information.
- **All staff** handle information according to its label.

## Compliance

Handling is checked during the annual data discovery scan.

## Exceptions

To be agreed.

## Review

Reviewed annually once approved.
