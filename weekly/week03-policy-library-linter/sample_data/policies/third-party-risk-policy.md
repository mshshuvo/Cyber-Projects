# Third-Party Risk Management Policy

## Purpose

To identify, assess and monitor risks from suppliers and service providers, in line with
OSFI Guideline B-10.

## Scope

All third-party arrangements, including cloud services and fourth parties used by critical suppliers.

## Policy statements

1. Every new arrangement is risk-rated before contract signature.
2. Critical suppliers provide a SOC 2 Type II report, or equivalent assurance, every year.
3. Contracts include audit rights, incident notification within 24 hours and exit provisions.
4. Concentration risk is reported to the CRO quarterly.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Vendor Manager** owns this policy and the third-party inventory.
- **Business owners** manage each relationship.

## Compliance

Internal audit tests a sample of arrangements annually.

## Exceptions

Exceptions are approved by the CRO.

## Review

Reviewed annually.
