# Information Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets the direction for protecting Maple Ridge Credit Union's Information Assets
and the Personal Information of its members. It is the top-level document of the information
security management system and is supported by the topic-specific policies listed below.
Terms in this policy have the meanings in the [glossary](glossary.md).

## Scope

All employees, contractors and third parties who access credit union Information Assets, at
all branches and in all hosted environments.

## Policy statements

1. Information security objectives are set annually by the CISO and approved by the Board Risk Committee.
2. Access is granted on least privilege and need to know, as set out in the Access Control Policy (POL-AC-001).
3. Information is classified and handled according to POL-DC-001.
4. Cryptographic controls follow STD-CR-001.
5. Every Security Incident is reported and handled under POL-IR-001.
6. Acceptable use of credit union systems is governed by POL-AU-001.
7. The program is aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, and
   meets OSFI expectations for technology and cyber risk.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Board Risk Committee** approves this policy and receives the annual security report.
- **CISO** owns this policy and the security program.
- **All staff** comply with this policy and the supporting documents.

## Compliance

Compliance is monitored through internal audit and the control testing program. Breaches may
lead to disciplinary action.

## Exceptions

Exceptions are requested through the risk acceptance process, approved by the CRO and
recorded in the risk register with an expiry date.

## Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually, and after any significant change to the
business, threat landscape or regulatory requirements.
