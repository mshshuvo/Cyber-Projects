# Business Continuity Policy

## Purpose

To keep critical member services running, or restore them within agreed targets, after a
disruption.

## Scope

All critical business processes and the technology that supports them.

## Policy statements

1. Every critical process has a BIA, refreshed annually, using the [BIA procedure](procedures/bia-procedure.md).
2. RTO and RPO targets are approved by the process owner and the CRO.
3. Recovery plans are tested at least annually, including one full failover of core banking.
4. Backups are immutable and restore-tested quarterly.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Head of Business Continuity** owns this policy and the test program.
- **Process owners** maintain recovery plans.

## Compliance

Test results and gaps are reported to the Chief Risk Officer.

## Exceptions

Exceptions follow the Information Security Policy (POL-IS-001).

## Review

Reviewed annually.
