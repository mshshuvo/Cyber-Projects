# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose

To set clear rules for using credit union devices, email, internet and messaging.

## Scope

All employees and contractors, on credit union devices and on personal devices used for work.

## Policy statements

1. Credit union systems are for business use; limited personal use is allowed if it does not
   interfere with work or breach any policy.
2. Member Personal Information must not be sent to personal email or unapproved cloud storage.
3. Remote work must follow the Remote Access Policy (POL-RA-001) and use the corporate VPN.
4. Report lost or stolen devices to the service desk at [Insert helpdesk extension] immediately.
5. Staff sign this policy at onboarding and re-acknowledge it annually.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Head of HR** owns this policy and tracks acknowledgements.
- **Managers** make sure their teams have acknowledged it.

## Compliance

Breaches are handled through the disciplinary process.

## Review

Reviewed annually.
